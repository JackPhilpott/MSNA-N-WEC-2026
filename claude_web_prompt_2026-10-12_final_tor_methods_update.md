## 1. What changed, at a glance

1. **Two analysis rounds.**
   - **Round 1:** everything submitted by 30 Sep. It has been cleaned and analysed, and its representativity summary has gone to the IPC/CH process.
   - **Round 2:** cumulative, from the first interview to the end of collection (end date [Jack to confirm]).
   - Each round has its own representativity rule (section 6).
2. **Round 1 analysis covers only part of the design.** It covers the North-East (Adamawa, Borno, Yobe) and North-West (Katsina, Sokoto, Zamfara, plus Kaduna's two design LGAs, Jema'a and Zaria, with no Kaduna state figure). Kebbi and all North-Central states were collected but are outside the Round 1 analysis.
3. **Accessibility was managed throughout fieldwork, in every region.**
   - Partners reported access at ward and cluster level; inaccessible sample points were replaced from the stratum's accessible remainder.
   - 19 strata in 12 LGAs were dropped when their accessible population fell below the threshold.
   - Estimates represent the accessible population of each stratum.
4. **Border buffer revised:**
   - the Niger border buffer went from 20 km to 5 km in the North-West (6 Aug, 24 LGAs);
   - two Mobbar wards inside the 20 km buffer were reopened on partner-confirmed access (3 Sep).
5. **IDP sampling unit changed from hexagon to DTM site** for every IDP cluster drawn from 2 September. It was not applied retroactively.
6. **Replacement and top-up mechanisms were formalised:**
   - supplementary cluster draws (Tier 1 / Tier 2);
   - extra interviews at certainty sites;
   - depth top-ups (IDP from 27 Sep; Non-IDP on 6 Oct);
   - 323 spare clusters (5 Oct);
   - standing rules on when to draw and how far.
7. **MSNA Light:** a government-negotiated collection with no probability guarantee, in four Borno Non-IDP strata (Abadam, Guzamala, Nganzai, and Ngala Non-IDP from 29 Sep).
8. **Partner coverage changed:**
   - LGAs were reallocated on 25 Sep (8 LGAs) and 4 Oct (17 LGAs);
   - 19 organisations collected household data.
9. **Cleaning rules were made explicit and uniform:** the definition of an achieved interview, the 20-minute duration rule, and the partner recovery workbooks and deletion log.
10. **Representativity is measured on the accessible population.** Round 1 uses a simplified rule (design effect 1); Round 2 uses the strict cluster-aware rule. Categories (a)–(d), with LGA and state aggregation.
11. **Round 1 weights are final and independently verified.** Round 2 weights are not yet built.
12. **Sample figures have changed** (section 2).

## 3 (continued). The changes in detail (for 3.5, 3.7, 3.8 and Annex 1)

### 3.1 Replacement and additional sampling
- **Supplementary draws:** where clusters became inaccessible or delivered too few households, new clusters were drawn from the stratum's remaining accessible pool by the same PPS method, in successive rounds through September and October.
  - Tier 1 draws fresh units only. Tier 2 allows repeat draws once the fresh pool is exhausted.
  - From 21 Sep, Non-IDP draws used **building-validated pools**: only hexagons with enough eligible buildings for a full cluster, and only households in accessible wards.
- **Standing rules (decided by the MSNA lead):**
  - **29 Sep:** keep drawing (Tier 1, then Tier 2) until each non-Representative stratum's pool is exhausted, unless the target would grow so much that it becomes a resource question.
  - **30 Sep:** additional sampling aims for 9–9.5% MoE, not just under 10%.
  - **4 Oct, the "150 rule":** top up a non-Representative stratum only if the extra interviews needed are 150 or fewer. Aim for 9.25%; if that isn't reachable within 150, aim for 10%; otherwise skip.
- **Certainty sites (21 Sep):** extra interviews at IDP sites selected with certainty were accepted as a way to close a stratum.
- **Depth top-ups:**
  - IDP strata from 27–30 Sep: extra interviews in existing sites.
  - Non-IDP on 6 Oct: 648 primary + 648 reserve households added inside 40 existing clusters in 6 strata (FACT: Malumfashi, Dandume, Dikwa, Yauri, Damboa; INTERSOS: Magumeri).
  - Topped-up clusters keep their ID; new households continue the cluster's numbering.
- **4 Oct round:**
  - IDP top-ups in 4 strata (Madagali, Monguno, Maru, Gwadabawa);
  - new Non-IDP clusters under the 150 rule: 66 clusters in 18 strata, out of 165 requested in 21 strata (the pools delivered less than requested).
- **Spare clusters (5 Oct):**
  - 323 in 165 strata (242 Non-IDP + 81 IDP), up to 2 per stratum still collecting;
  - 158 strata got 2, 7 got 1, 93 got none (pool exhausted);
  - partners use a spare only if a cluster in that LGA can't be completed, in spare-rank order;
  - unused spares are not part of any target, MoE or weight.

### 3.3 IDP sampling unit (2 Sep)
- **Problem:** the July design selected 5 km hexagons and surveyed only the largest DTM site in each. 556 of 1,640 candidate IDP hexagons held more than one site, and 237,312 households in the smaller co-located sites had zero chance of selection.
- **Change:** from 2 Sep, every new IDP cluster is an individual DTM site selected by PPS on site households: a sequential PPS draw without replacement per batch, from accessible sites, excluding sites within 30 m of an existing cluster.
- It was not applied retroactively. Each cluster is tagged hexagon or site, and the weights combine the two designs (section 3.7 of this brief).
- The frame now holds 1,226 hexagon-method and 647 site-level IDP clusters.

### 3.5 Data processing rules (all fieldwork)
- **Daily pipeline:**
  - the data officer's anonymised KoBo export;
  - each interview matched to the sampled point it claims (when two interviews claim one point, the first keeps it);
  - deletion decisions applied;
  - a daily monitoring dashboard and daily partner to-do lists (workbooks and maps) refreshed from the same data;
  - the data officer's cleaning pipeline produces the analysis dataset.
- **Achieved:** completed, matched to a sampled point, and not a settled deletion (confirmed, or contested and upheld). The same definition is used by the dashboard, partner workbooks, the frame refresh, representativity and weights.
  - Pending flags never remove an interview (10–11 Sep).
  - Oversampled interviews count (20 Sep).
  - A zero food consumption score is a flag, not a deletion (10 Sep).
  - Interviews with a wrong device date are kept and flagged.
- **Deletion reasons:**
  - **Duration:** removed only if the audit-trail duration, rounded to one decimal, is below 20.0 minutes (that is, under 19.95; 2 Oct).
  - **No consent.**
  - **Partner recovery-workbook outcomes:** duplicate household, or sampling information that is missing or can't be recovered. Partners could appeal each flag.
  - Round 1 deletion log for the data officer: 6,533 rows (2,599 removals, 2,981 corrections, 953 flags kept).
- **Audit gap (Round 2):** interviews whose KoBo audit log hasn't been received yet count as achieved until it arrives, and are then checked against the duration rule.

### 3.7 Rounds, representativity and weights
- **Round 1 membership:**
  - assigned by processed date: every record in the anonymised export at the close of Round 1 (28,046 submissions);
  - the final cleaned dataset uses a 30 Sep submission-date cut-off (the 79 submitted 1 Oct are Round 2).
- **Round 2:** cumulative to the end of collection.
- **Round 1 rule (1 Oct, after an HQ technical meeting; applied uniformly to every stratum):**
  - MoE = 1.6449 × √(0.25 / n_eff) × 100, with n_eff = n (N − 1) / (N − n);
  - n = interviews; N = accessible households; 90% confidence; p = 0.5; design effect 1; finite-population correction.
- **Round 2 rule (strict):** the same formula with a cluster design effect, deff = 1 + ((CV² + 1) × m̄ − 1) × ICC, ICC = 0.06.
  - IDP strata where certainty sites hold at least 90% of the accessible population get certainty-site treatment (a site needs at least 3 interviews).
  - The certainty-site treatment was dropped for Round 1, because it changes no verdict under design effect 1.
- **Strata that lost access after their interviews were collected (2 Oct)** are included in Round 1, assessed against the larger accessible share recorded during collection (8 or 26 Sep). Gubio IDP, never recorded as accessible, uses all its households (conservative).
- **LGA and state figures:**
  - computed over (a) and (b) strata only, each weighted by its share of accessible households: MoE = 1.6449 × √(Σ W² × Var) × 100;
  - (c) and (d) strata are left out of every aggregate;
  - each aggregate states the share of households it represents;
  - combined IDP + Non-IDP figures rely on the ToR assumption that WorldPop Non-IDP estimates exclude IDPs.
- **Round 1 weights.** The ToR needs the method and theory only (Jack, 12 Oct). Don't quote weighted totals or weight statistics. Final 2 Oct; verified independently:
  - base weight = 1 / (P(cluster) × P(household | cluster)), using the probability recorded at each draw;
  - **second stage** = interviews / households in the unit, so it adjusts for both over- and under-collection;
  - **Non-IDP:** repeat draws of one hexagon in one stratum are pooled into one weighting unit;
  - **IDP, "Option 3":**
    - in strata with only July clusters, the surveyed site stands for its hexagon;
    - in mixed strata, a two-phase site-level probability;
    - post-2 Sep site probabilities are combined across draw batches, rebuilt from preserved site-frame snapshots (a single-pool approximation in 26 strata);
  - weights trimmed at 4 × the stratum median, then calibrated so each stratum sums to its accessible households;
  - not weighted: MSNA Light and Dropped strata.
- **Round 1 analysis design:** strata and weights, no cluster term: `svydesign(ids = ~1, strata = ~strata_id, weights = ~weight)`.
  - A Representative stratum can show a weighted MoE above 10%, because the Round 1 rule has no weighting term. Footnote this.
- **Round 2 weights:** not yet built **[Jack to confirm the plan]**.

  - every other LGA's sample was left exactly as it was;
  - effect: 5 North-West LGAs gained an IDP stratum (Sandamu, Illela, Baure, Jibia, Mai'adua).
- **3 Sep:**
  - Damasak and Zanna Umarti wards (Mobbar, Borno) were reopened inside the 20 km buffer after the partner confirmed access;
  - this created Mobbar's IDP stratum (15,913 households, dominated by Gsss Camp Damasak, 12,243) and added 10,919 Non-IDP households in 16 hexagons.

### 3.9 Limitations to add (section 3.8 / A1.4)
1. **Estimates represent the accessible population** of each stratum. Round 1 states represent 50–82% of their households (section 2.3).
2. **Round 1 has reduced coverage:**
   - no Round 1 estimates for Kebbi or the North-Central states, although data exist;
   - Kaduna is reported for 2 LGAs only, which hold 9.5% of its households.
3. **Access changed during fieldwork:** replacements came from each stratum's accessible remainder, and 19 strata were dropped.
4. **Partner changes mid-fieldwork:** reallocations moved LGAs between teams, so team effects within an LGA are possible.
5. **IDP design change on 2 Sep:**
   - two selection designs are combined in the weights;
   - in 26 strata the post-2 Sep pools were reconstructed rather than recorded;
   - in July-only strata, co-located sites are represented by the surveyed site;
   - the IDP second stage uses DTM provisional household estimates.
6. **Concentration from repeat draws and depth top-ups:** planned precision can be optimistic where many interviews sit in few places. The weights pool repeat draws.
7. **MSNA Light** has no verifiable probability design. It is LGA-level only and not weighted.
8. **The Round 1 rule ignores clustering and weighting.** Weighted margins can exceed the 10% label (footnoted). On the same data, the strict rule gives 84 Representative strata against 216.
9. **Weight trimming** at 4 × the median trades a small bias for lower variance.
10. **Second-household encoding:** when two households were interviewed at one sampled point, the second carries a `_b` suffix. Row counts include both.
11. **GPS-outlier matches:** a share of interviews were recorded away from their matched point (as of 21 Sep: 24% of IDP and 18% of Non-IDP matches were GPS outliers). **[Jack to decide whether to disclose; data-quality review ongoing.]**

## 6. Accuracy guardrails

- **Never describe Round 1 as covering all 11 states.** Round 1 analysis is the North-East and North-West excluding Kebbi; Kaduna has LGA figures only.
- **Don't mix the bases:**
  - 25,447 (Round 1 achieved) and 21,705 (in coverage) are monitoring counts of 2 Oct;
  - 21,428 and 20,858 come from the final cleaned dataset, the basis of the IPC/CH summary.
  
  Use the cleaned-dataset figures for Round 1 results unless Jack says otherwise.
- **"Representative" in Round 1 means under the simplified Round 1 rule.** Say so wherever the word appears.
- **"Excluded" is not "not covered":**
  - excluded = dropped for access, the certainty rule or no remaining population;
  - not covered = no partner.
  
  For both, no estimate is possible.
- **MSNA Light is not a probability sample.** Never aggregate it, and never call it representative of the LGA.
- **"Target" has several definitions.** Always name which one: July plan, 6 Aug plan, the fielded design target, or the target in collected clusters.
- **Don't introduce numbers that aren't in this brief.** No new MoE ranges, cluster totals or percentages.
