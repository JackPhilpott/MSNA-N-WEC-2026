**Subject: NGA MSNA 2026 — your data collection package has been significantly updated**

Dear [Partner organisation] team,

Your NGA MSNA 2026 partner folder — the same one you already have access to, no new
link needed — has just been significantly updated. It now contains everything your
enumeration teams need for every cluster assigned to you: exact GPS points,
cluster-specific field guides, and orientation maps, replacing the earlier pilot
KML-only files.

**The folder structure has also changed** — each LGA folder is now split by
population group, so if your team has already started browsing the old layout, note
the new structure below before your next visit.

**What's in your folder now**

Your folder (`[Partner]/`) is organised by **State → LGA**, and each LGA folder is
split by population group (Non-IDP / IDP), each with its own GPS points and
cluster-specific field guides:

```
[Partner]/<State>/<LGA>/
  [LGA_name]_map.png              - whole-LGA overview
  Non_IDP/
    KML/                          - household GPS points (primary + reserve)
    Cluster_guide/                - one field-guide document per cluster
  IDP/
    KML/                          - site GPS points (primary + Tier 2 backup)
    Cluster_guide/                - one field-guide document per cluster
```

At the top level of your folder you'll also find:
- **`How_To_Use_This_Package.pdf`** (updated) — read this first. It explains the
  folder structure above in full, how to open the KML files on a phone or computer,
  and the key rules your teams need before going to the field (primary-before-reserve,
  Tier 1/Tier 2 for IDP camps, the accepted GPS-offset range if a household isn't
  exactly at the pin, and more).
- **`MSNA_NGA_MapsMe_Guide.pdf`** (new) — a fully illustrated, step-by-step guide to
  installing Maps.me and importing/navigating to your KML points, with annotated
  phone screenshots. If your team hasn't set up Maps.me yet, start here.
- **`NGA_MSNA_2026_Data_Collection_SOP.pdf`** — who to contact and how, depending
  on the urgency of the issue (safety/security, operational, or data quality).
- **`[Partner]_sampling_points_summary.xlsx`** (updated) — every GPS point assigned
  to you, in one spreadsheet, if your team prefers a table view over opening
  individual KML files or field guides.

**Each cluster's field guide** (`Cluster_guide/[cluster_id]_factsheet.docx`) is a
self-contained document: the cluster's exact location and task instructions, target
and reserve counts, an interview log grid, and — at the end — a detailed cluster map
(roads, buildings, sample points, and any known nearby landmarks) plus a wider
LGA-context map. The first two pages of every field guide are a general reference
section (replacement rules, what to do if a household isn't exactly at the GPS pin,
who to contact) — the same two pages repeat in every cluster's document, so each
one works on its own in the field without needing anything else printed alongside it.

**Before your teams go to the field**

Please make sure whoever is briefing enumerators has read the updated
`How_To_Use_This_Package.pdf` in full — it covers several things that have come up
already during the pilot phase (in particular, what to do when a household isn't
exactly at the GPS pin, and the difference between Tier 1 and Tier 2 for IDP camps).
If your team has already downloaded or printed material from the old folder layout,
please treat this update as the current version and discard anything from before.

If anything in your folder looks incomplete, incorrect, or missing for an LGA you're
covering, please flag it using the contact tiers in the SOP rather than assuming it's
intentional — we'd rather confirm than have a team working from a gap.

Please confirm you can see the update, and let us know if you have any questions.

Best regards,
[Jack Philpott]
IMPACT Initiatives / FACT Foundation — NGA MSNA 2026
