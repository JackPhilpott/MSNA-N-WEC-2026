Subject: Updated NGA MSNA 2026 data collection package — what's new

Dear partners,

Please find your updated data collection package attached/linked. This replaces the
version shared previously — please use this one going forward. A summary of what's in
it and what's changed is below.

**What's in your package (unchanged structure):**
- A folder per State → LGA you're covering, each with your GPS points (Non-IDP
  household primary/reserve, IDP cluster primary, IDP Tier 2 backup where applicable)
  as KML files for Maps.me/Google Earth, plus an LGA overview map.
- Your Excel summary workbook (`[Partner]_sampling_points_summary.xlsx`) with every
  point in one table, plus your targeted sample by State/LGA.
- A short "How to use this package" PDF at the top level, explaining what everything is
  and how to open it.

**What's new today:**

1. **Every cluster now has its own field sheet** (`[cluster_id]_factsheet.docx`, inside
   each LGA folder) — one page per cluster with its location, your task for that
   cluster (household list / Tier 1–Tier 2 / chief listing, as applicable), target and
   reserve counts, any relevant flags, a household interview log grid, and — new — two
   larger maps at the end: a close-up cluster-level map (hexagon/point layout, roads,
   buildings) and a wider LGA-context map showing where that cluster sits.
2. **Clearer guidance on three things field teams have flagged as recurring points of
   confusion**, now emphasised directly in each factsheet and the general field guide:
   - Ward is approximate and for orientation only — your GPS point is anchored to the
     LGA, not the ward boundary.
   - Reserve points are a backup only — using them beyond the standard replacement
     rules needs prior approval from your FACT Foundation/IMPACT Initiatives focal
     point.
   - Full household listing is mandatory for every IDP cluster (in-camp and
     host-community alike) — without it, that cluster's data cannot be accepted.
3. **LGA and Ward data sources are now labelled explicitly** in your Excel workbook.
   LGA is always OCHA/COD (the authoritative source). Ward is GRID3 (the only
   national-coverage ward product) — and for North-East clusters, OCHA/COD's own ward
   product is now also shown alongside it for cross-reference. These two ward sources
   occasionally disagree by a small margin right at LGA borders; this is a known,
   expected discrepancy between two real datasets, not an error, and never changes
   which LGA a point belongs to. Full explanation in your workbook's README sheet.

**What hasn't changed:** your assigned LGAs, your targeted sample sizes, and the
GPS points themselves are all the same as before — this update is about clearer
supporting material, not a change to your sample.

Please replace your previous copy of the package with this one. If anything looks
inconsistent with what you had before, or you spot an issue in the field, let us know
via your usual focal point.

Thank you for your continued work on this assessment.
